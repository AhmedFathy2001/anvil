## Highlights
- Clan pages now show what's live, the clan's own leaderboard and what's coming up, each one click away.
- Invite friends to Anvil Supporter and earn free months, with bonus rewards at 3, 10 and 25 friends.

## New
- What's new in Anvil lives at anvilosrs.com/changelog, and clans can get it in Discord with the new "Anvil updates" channel setting.
- Clan admins can search every setting from the admin area with Ctrl+K.
- Each event's Discord tab lists the channels and roles Anvil made for it, with buttons to create missing ones, re-sync roles or clean up.
- A Plan and billing page in the clan admin.
- Champion and Patron supporters can pick their name's gradient, and Patrons can choose their own colours. It shows on the site, in the RuneLite panel and on Discord posts.

## Improved
- Supporter names show their colours on clan member lists, member pages and event standings.
- The home page's live competition card opens the competition and shows the leaders before you've scored.
- Searching stat tile standings on large boards no longer lags.
- Easier-on-the-eyes buttons in Discord role settings.
