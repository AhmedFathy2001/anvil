## News
Anvil chat is an opt-in way to keep talking to signed-in Anvil players across public, clan, friends
chat, guest clan and private messages. Event chat can now live in the RuneLite chatbox too, with its
channel tag, colours and a quick reply action.

Settings have been reorganised around the things you are trying to change, with in-game chat colours
easy to find and preview. Hidden-progress tile races now keep unrevealed steps out of shared Discord
posts while still showing useful progress in the side panel and standings.

### New
- [Plugin] Opt-in Anvil chat and event chat inside the RuneLite chatbox.
- A clearer Account, Profile and In game settings layout.

### Improved
- Hidden tile races reveal useful progress without giving away what is ahead.
- Solo events automatically present shared entries as teams when needed.

## Highlights
- [Plugin] Anvil 1.17.0 adds opt-in cross-client chat, event chat in the game chatbox and right-click connected status.
- Settings are grouped by purpose, with chat colours promoted to the In game page and a stronger readable default.
- Hidden-progress tile races protect unrevealed steps in shared Discord channels, the side panel and standings.

## New
- [Plugin] Opt-in Anvil chat for public, clan, friends chat, guest clan and private messages between eligible signed-in players.
- [Plugin] Event chat in the game chatbox, with channel tags, colours and a reply hotkey.
- [Plugin] Right-click a player or chat name to see whether they can receive Anvil chat; only lines actually relayed through Anvil carry its icon.
- Settings reorganised into focused Account, Profile and In game pages, with live previews where appearance matters.
- Chat colours are a first-class section under Settings → In game and remain beside the profile-look preview.
- Supporter name looks can be animated, still or off in chat and elsewhere.

## Improved
- [Plugin] The side panel has better boss icons, badge alignment, wrapped rows, animation timing and macOS symbol fallbacks.
- [Plugin] Ordinary Anvil information lines use the site-controlled high-contrast colour instead of inheriting a faint RuneLite colour.
- Hidden tile races show safe step progress in standings and the side panel without exposing locked tile names.
- Solo standings stay player-based for one-person entries and automatically become team-based when an entry gains more members.
- Clicking a one-person entry opens that player directly; clicking a shared entry opens its team roster.
- Board-reveal images use the Anvil mark for locked tiles and omit meaningless zero-point labels.

## Fixed
- Shared Discord channels no longer reveal hidden race steps through completion posts, digests or summaries.
- Money and Analytics are restricted to the platform root account rather than clan owners.
- [Plugin] Chat relay deduplication, reconnect handling, resource cleanup and performance issues found during the release audit.
