## New
- Search every admin setting with Ctrl+K: type what you want ("deaths", "dink", "boosts", "1/128") and it takes you straight to the setting and highlights it. It forgives typos, shows where each setting lives, and lists what's new and popular before you type.
- Challenges have their own place in the menu.

## Improved
- Clan settings shows its sections (Profile, Features, Rules & scoring, Boosts) right in the admin sidebar.
- Discord settings and Rules & scoring link to each other, so what the clan feed keeps is easy to find.
- Each event has a Discord item in its admin menu, and the event's Discord panel can copy the join link.
- Weekly competitions have a Settings button on their page.
- The guides match where everything is now, with new sections on clan boosts, game modes, old webhooks and Post to Discord, in every language.
- [Plugin] New rows in the Feed tab show their GZ, Sad and Sit straight away.
