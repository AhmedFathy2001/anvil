## Improved
- When someone dies several times in a row, the feed shows it once with a count ("Died to Vorkath ×12") instead of a row per death, in the plugin's Feed tab and on the site.
- Pets in the Feed show their picture, including ones from before.
- Deaths are kept in every clan's feed by default.

## Fixed
- Deaths no longer read "Died to Death" after dying at bosses like Phosani's Nightmare.
- The explanations in the Supporter page's comparison table are no longer cut off.
- The Feed's drop floor line no longer shows a broken character.
- The "Keep deaths too" setting describes where deaths show.
