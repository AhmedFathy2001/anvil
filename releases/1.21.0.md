## News
Competitions grew up: members can sign themselves up, a week can cover several bosses or skills, there
is an item hunt, and each one posts a daily update. Every competition now has its own feed, and a
recap when it ends with the week in numbers, who died where and who got lucky.

Tile races can carry easier options that open over time or when a team spends a lifeline. Event pages
open on your own team and what to go for next, with the board and standings side by side.

### New
- Competitions: sign-ups, entry fees, several bosses or skills in one, and item hunts.
- A feed and an end-of-week recap on every competition.
- Tile races: easier options, timed easing and lifelines.
- Speak or paste a tile list to build a board quickly.

### Improved
- Event pages lead with your team and your next tiles.
- Notable drop posts say where the drop came from, the kill count and the rate.

## Highlights
- Competitions take sign-ups and entry fees, can count several bosses or skills, and post a daily update and halfway standings to Discord.
- Every competition has Standings and Feed tabs, and a Recap once it ends.
- Tile races can offer easier options, on a timer for everyone or through team lifelines.
- Event, competition and clan event pages share one layout that starts with what matters to you.

## New
- Competitions: a "People sign up" choice, so members enter their own character from the competition's page until it ends.
- Competitions: an optional entry fee in gp, paid in game. Fees can be added to the prizes, staff can remind everyone who owes, and prizes can wait while a winner still owes theirs.
- Competitions: one weekly can count up to ten bosses or skills, summed, with each player's split by boss.
- Item hunts: a competition for a drop, won by the first or by the most, with luckiest and driest shown when it ends.
- Competitions: a daily update in Discord with the biggest day and the top five, and halfway standings for solo competitions. Each has its own switch on the Discord page.
- Competitions: a Feed tab kept to that competition's own bosses or skill. It shows drops, deaths rolled up per person per day, changes in the top places and a daily update.
- Competitions: a Recap when one ends, with your own week, the week in numbers, deaths by boss, drops and luck, and the awards.
- Best drops on every competition.
- Tile races: a tile can have easier options the host writes up front. They can open for everyone as time passes, or for one team when it spends a lifeline.
- Tile race road editor: save an unfinished road as a draft, a warning before saving over someone else's changes, and one button to remove tiles left off the road.
- Quick add: speak your tile list, and name a tile on its own line.
- Kills and Skill XP tiles can count each boss or skill separately, all of them or at least a set number.
- Tile editor: choose a tile's picture yourself.
- Game modes: boards that only count a game mode, and a mode tag on proofs.
- Clans can set default tiers for combat task, quest and diary posts; members follow them until they choose their own.
- Members page: luck for this week or all time, and a switch to leave guests off.
- Sign-ups can keep taking plugin members after an event has started.

## Improved
- Event pages show the whole banner beside the title, then a "Your team" bar with your place and the tiles closest to done, then the board with standings beside it.
- Tile race pages lead with your next step and how far ahead or behind you are.
- Clan event pages have the same header, one clear button to say you are going or to sign up, and who is in beside the details.
- Notable drop posts carry the source, the kill count and the drop rate when they are known. The lucky line shows only when the numbers back it.
- Hours on the Members page are more accurate: Slayer, Wintertodt, Tempoross, Zalcano, Sailing and Bryophyta are counted properly.
- Clan pages have scenery behind Moments and live competition cards, using the game's own icons in your clan's colour.
- Proofs: paste an image, enter an amount on big-stack drop tiles, and staff can correct one without a screenshot.
- Combo tiles: each part has its own upload form and lists its own items, in the side panel too.
- Hovering a tile on a race map shows every road that leaves it.
- Pricing and Billing let a clan owner pick the clan and any tier that fits.
- [Plugin] Title-bar buttons and name colours no longer flicker when you switch tabs or resize the collection log.
- [Plugin] On a narrow collection log, Sync to Anvil moves into the log's own menu.
- [Plugin] A relayed clan chat line keeps the sender's rank icon.

## Fixed
- A pet shows once in recent drops, not as the pet and the item.
- A combo part finishing counts as progress, with one post, not a completed step.
- Competitions over several bosses start late entrants from the right number.
- People banned from the platform no longer show on public pages.
- The page scrolls again after one pop-up closes over another.
- The recruiting card no longer says applications are closed when they are open.
- [Plugin] A rejected token is reported in chat straight away.
- [Plugin] Turning Supporter colours off or on updates the friends, friends chat and clan lists at once.
- [Plugin] An ended event stops tracking without signing you out.
