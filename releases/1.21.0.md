## News
Item hunt is a new kind of competition: pick an item (a pet, a unique, anything that drops) and the
clan races to get it. The first one wins, or whoever gets the most by the end. Competitions also take
sign-ups and entry fees now, can count several bosses or skills, and each has its own feed and a
recap when it ends.

Event, competition and clan event pages have been redesigned. Each opens on your own team and what to
go for next, with the board and standings side by side and the full feed beside the chat. The whole
site also got a pass for phones.

Joining is simpler: name your character on the sign-up page and get in through the plugin, an XP
check, or a staff vouch, and an invite link takes one press. Hours are now counted at an efficient
pace for every boss and skill, so your hours will read lower than before.

### New
- Item hunt: a new competition for a drop, won by the first or by the most.
- Competitions: sign-ups, entry fees, several bosses or skills in one, a feed and an end-of-week recap.
- Tile races: easier options that open over time or with a team lifeline.
- Join a clan or an event with your character named on the spot: plugin, XP check or staff vouch.
- Download your data or delete your account from Settings.
- Guides that walk owners, staff and new members through setting up, running an event and getting started.

### Improved
- Redesigned event, competition and clan event pages that lead with your team and your next tiles.
- A full pass for phones: menus, boards, tap sizes and the admin pages.
- Hours at an efficient pace for every boss and skill; expect lower numbers.
- Notable drop posts say where the drop came from, the kill count and the rate.

### Fixed
- Tile progress no longer keeps counting after an event ends or a tile closes.
- [Plugin] Anvil chat icons, rank icons on relayed lines, and flicker in the chat and clan lists.
- [Plugin] Drops are no longer lost if you close the client before they are sent.

## Highlights
- Item hunt is a new competition mode: the clan races for a drop.

## New
- Item hunt, a new competition mode: choose the item and where it drops, and whether the first drop wins or the most by the end. It can end on a date, after a set number of drops, or when one player reaches a number.
- Item hunt drops count through the plugin, or by a screenshot with the kill count or XP that staff approve. The hunt's page lists every drop with what the player was on, and shows the luckiest and driest when it ends.
- Competitions: a "People sign up" choice, so members enter their own character from the competition's page until it ends.
- Competitions: an optional entry fee in gp, paid in game. Fees can be added to the prizes, staff can remind everyone who owes, and prizes can wait while a winner still owes theirs.
- Competitions: one weekly can count up to ten bosses or skills, summed, with each player's split by boss. You can also start one with selected people only.
- Competitions: prizes show on the competition's page and in the plugin panel, and what it counts can be changed before it starts.
- Competitions: a daily update in Discord with the biggest day and the top five, and halfway standings for solo competitions. Each has its own switch on the Discord page.
- Competitions: a Feed tab kept to that competition's own bosses or skill. It shows drops, deaths rolled up per person per day, changes in the top places and a daily update.
- Competitions: a Recap when one ends, with your own week, the week in numbers, deaths by boss, drops and luck, and the awards.
- Best drops on every competition, on its page, in the results post and in the plugin panel.
- A pet or drop in a single-skill or single-boss competition shows the XP or kill count it landed on.
- Tile races: a tile can have easier options the host writes up front. They can open for everyone as time passes, or for one team when it spends a lifeline. Lifelines can be spent from the site or the plugin panel.
- Tile race road editor: save an unfinished road as a draft, a warning before saving over someone else's changes, and one button to remove tiles left off the road.
- Joining: name your character on the sign-up or apply page and prove it by the plugin, an XP check, or a staff vouch. Plugin and XP-checked characters get a guest seat at once; a vouch waits for staff.
- Invite links: one Join button seats you and signs you up, and an invite can admit someone who is not on the roster. Late joiners can be invited to a running event.
- After linking a character you are taken back to what you were doing, with a Continue button.
- Settings → Account → Your data: download everything Anvil holds about you, or delete your account.
- Guides on the page: a run-an-event guide on every event admin page, a new-owner guide on the dashboard and setup wizard, a moderator guide, and getting-started strips for members and for players with no clan.
- A home for players with no clan: recruiting clans, public boards and your own setup progress.
- Quick add: speak your tile list, name a tile on its own line, and undo the last Quick add.
- Kills and Skill XP tiles can count each boss or skill separately, all of them or at least a set number.
- Tile editor: choose a tile's picture yourself, and move the open tile earlier or later.
- Game modes: boards that only count a game mode, a mode tag on proofs, and a switch for the catch-all congratulation lines.
- Clans can set default tiers for combat task, quest and diary posts; members follow them until they choose their own.
- Members page: luck for this week or all time, a marker for who was active this week, and a switch to leave guests off.
- Sign-ups can keep taking plugin members after an event has started, and staff can approve all pending sign-ups at once.
- Challenges: a list of who you blocked, with Unblock on each.
- Discord page: a card listing everything each slash command can be asked, with what your server allows for each.
- [Plugin] On a narrow collection log, Sync to Anvil moves into the log's own menu.

## Improved
- Event pages show the whole banner beside the title, then a "Your team" bar with your place and the tiles closest to done, then the board with standings beside it.
- The Board tab has one short Latest strip. The full feed sits beside the chat on Feed & chat, and Firsts and Luck moved to Stats.
- Tile race pages lead with your next step and how far ahead or behind you are.
- Clan event pages have the same header, one clear button to say you are going or to sign up, and who is in beside the details.
- Phones: a scrollable menu, larger tap targets, no text under 12px, the board first on event pages, tab strips that show there is more, and admin lists that turn into cards.
- Hours are counted at an efficient pace for every boss and skill, so nobody is credited more hours than they played. Expect your hours to read lower than before.
- The Members podium covers the last 7 days and says so.
- Board: a hover card on each tile with the count and who on your team is going for it, a toast when a team completes a line, and a tile's dialog you can link to.
- Events page and home: one "You're in" list of everything you are playing, with where you stand on each.
- A finished event has one Wrapping up list: prizes, recap, survey, run again, archive.
- Standings show the points needed to pass the place above, and your own row is pinned and marked in tables.
- Draft watch page: the current round and its order, picks until your team, and a pick clock that shows hours and days.
- Notable drop posts carry the source, the kill count and the drop rate when they are known. The lucky line shows only when the numbers back it.
- Clan pages have scenery behind Moments and live competition cards, using the game's own icons in your clan's colour. The public clan home shows events before Discord.
- Proofs: paste an image, enter an amount on big-stack drop tiles, and staff can correct one without a screenshot.
- Combo tiles: each part has its own upload form and lists its own items, in the side panel too, and staff see each part's proofs.
- Hovering a tile on a race map shows every road that leaves it.
- Forms that hold typed work ask before closing, and risky actions (paying winnings, declining a request, staff notices) ask you to confirm.
- Settings: tabs in setup order with a line on each, a "few that matter most" list, and plan and billing under Account.
- New clans start with a trial from Anvil, with a reminder three days before it ends. Pricing and Billing let a clan owner pick the clan and any tier that fits.
- Discord page: the setup block folds away once it is set up, and the event invite has Open and Copy buttons.
- A new plugin token is hidden until you press Reveal, and Copy works without showing it.
- Uploaded images have their hidden metadata removed.
- Public clan pages follow each character's "Show publicly" switch.
- Posting to a guest clan's channels is off until the player turns it on.
- Discord roles are matched by nickname only when the clan turns that on.
- [Plugin] Title-bar buttons and name colours no longer flicker when you switch tabs or resize the collection log.
- [Plugin] A relayed clan chat line keeps the sender's rank icon.
- [Plugin] The side panel's competition cards show prizes and no longer clip the clock or the name.

## Fixed
- A tile's kill or XP progress stops counting when the event ends or the tile closes, instead of adding what players did afterwards.
- A pet shows once in recent drops, not as the pet and the item.
- A combo part finishing counts as progress, with one post, not a completed step.
- Competitions over several bosses start late entrants from the right number.
- A locked race tile no longer accepts a hand-filed proof.
- A race map slightly wider than its box shrinks to fit instead of cutting off the finish.
- A partial refund no longer suspends the clan.
- People banned from the platform no longer show on public pages.
- The page scrolls again after one pop-up closes over another.
- The recruiting card no longer says applications are closed when they are open.
- Signing in returns you to the page you came from, inside the right clan.
- A proof the server cannot read is kept as sent instead of being lost, and a stalled proof no longer shows "Loading proof" forever.
- [Plugin] Anvil chat: the Anvil icon stays on the line it belongs to instead of appearing on a later line from someone else.
- [Plugin] Anvil chat: the connected status shows only for players who are sending through Anvil chat, and the two new chat lines no longer repeat the Anvil tag.
- [Plugin] Drops waiting to be sent are kept when you close the client, and each is credited once.
- [Plugin] A rejected token is reported in chat straight away, and signing out of the plugin revokes its token.
- [Plugin] Turning Supporter colours off or on updates the friends, friends chat and clan lists at once.
- [Plugin] An ended event stops tracking without signing you out.
