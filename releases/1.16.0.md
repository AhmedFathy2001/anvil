---
## Highlights
- Recent drops in the RuneLite panel are easier to read, and only show drops that clear your clan's rare-drop setting.
- Clan events can each post to their own Discord channel.
- Supporting Anvil is now one click away from any page and from the RuneLite panel.

## New
- Clan events can each post to their own Discord channel: pick it with "Post to" on the event form. The announcement, the 15-minute reminder and "starting now" all go there.
- A "Support Anvil" link in the site menu and footer, and at the bottom of the RuneLite panel.

## Improved
- [Plugin] Recent drops show the item and when on one line, and who got it with their GZs on the next, with more room between drops.
- [Plugin] Recent drops follow your clan's "Post drops rarer than 1 in…" Discord setting, so cheap drops off rare tables no longer fill the list. Drops worth 5m or more still show.
- [Plugin] Hovering a supporter's star shows their tier.
- [Plugin] Clicking a gold GZ when you aren't a supporter shows how to give them.
- Patron names on the site keep their gradient moving, and supporter stars wear the name's colours.
- Anvil Supporter sits higher on your profile page, above the webhook settings.
