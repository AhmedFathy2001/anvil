## News
The RuneLite side panel has been rebuilt from the ground up: every tab now tells you where you stand at a glance, what to do next, and lets you open any tile, board or player without hunting. If you're in several events at once, they're all tracked together.

Skill and Boss of the Week can now be run as teams, or clan against clan with as many clans as you like. Tile races got branching roads, and event chat has channels for your team, everyone, and staff announcements.

Clans can now set what members announce to Discord by default (drops, pets, slayer, clues, kill count milestones and more), with their own message wording, and players can still tune their own.

### New
- Team competitions and clan vs clan: any number of clans, each clan accepts, players can opt out
- Event chat channels: My team, All teams and Announcements
- Records for your best day, week, month and year, plus efficient hours
- Drop odds and luck: see how rare a drop is and whether you went dry or got spooned
- More announcement kinds: slayer, clues, speedruns, kill count milestones, BA gambles and your own custom messages

### Improved
- Drop announcements start at 1m or 1/1,000, and members follow their clan's settings unless they choose their own
- Every live board you're on is tracked at once in the plugin

## Highlights
- Side panel redesign: Clan, Feed, You, Lookup and Settings rebuilt with clear top cards, "Do this next", compact standings with rank movement, and a new profile header.
- Multi-board tracking: one drop credits every live board you're on, whether you're a member, a guest, invited, on a co-hosted board or in a creator event.
- Team competitions and clan vs clan for Skill/Boss of the Week.

## New
- Side panel: header cards with your banner and badge, a display case that shows the KC or luck behind each item, goals that name what you're chasing, a searchable collection log, and every board's full page from "Your live boards".
- Tile races: branching roads (any number), single start and finish, road labels, a race track in the panel, and padlocks on steps you haven't reached.
- Team competitions: split a competition into balanced teams, or race other clans; clans accept invites, choose all or selected members, and can block clans that spam invites.
- Event chat: My team, All teams and Announcements channels with unread counts; host and mods post announcements.
- Records: best day, week, month and year per skill and boss, on profiles and the clan's Members page.
- Efficient hours played and bossed, from OSRS Wiki rates, as a competition metric and on profiles.
- Drop odds on drop tiles (chance, expected kills, hours) and a spooned/dry read-out.
- Announcement kinds: slayer, clue scrolls, speedruns/PBs, kill count milestones, BA gambles, custom chat messages, and player-only Grand Exchange, trades and group storage.
- Message templates for every announcement kind, with a live preview.
- Per-kind screenshot switch for announcements (tile proofs always keep theirs).
- Milestones in the clan and board feeds, with their own filter.
- Clan event sign-ups from the panel: "I'm going" and "Register".
- Webhook pools for busy events, and staff tools to spot webhooks shared across clans.
- Guides: team competitions, drop odds, luck and records, tile race routes, and more, in every language.
- Unsaved-changes warnings on settings forms and the tile editor.
- Lab: real tiles for test sessions and a "Use real tiles" button.

## Improved
- Drop announcement defaults lowered to 1m / 1 in 1,000, inherited from your clan, with an either/both rule and per-webhook filters.
- Collection log, diary, quest and milestone rows appear on every live board feed you're on.
- Tile lists: new look with search, filters (including Not started and Closest first) and grouping by step or category.
- Rank movement (▲/▼) next to standings.
- Landing and About pages cover everything Anvil does.
- Clicking anywhere on a button, row or switch in the panel works, including its text.
- Release announcements are short news, with the full detail here on the changelog.

## Fixed
- Historical XP milestones could be posted for players who hadn't been checked in a while.
- Combat task and collection log proofs can be re-earned after a proof is deleted, without relogging.
- Tracked tiles that were deleted or locked no longer stay stuck on "loading".
- Clue tiles named by tier ("Clue Scroll (Hard)") now complete.
- Credits on co-hosted and test boards are no longer refused.
- Share images no longer crop items.
- Many security fixes across invites, private competitions, webhooks, guides and notices.
