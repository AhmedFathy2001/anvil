## New
- Supporters can choose a separate name colour for each linked RuneScape character. Champions can use up to two custom colours across their characters, while Patrons can give every character its own static colour or animated gradient.

## Improved
- Supporter colours remain readable across the site's dark surfaces and the plugin's light and transparent surfaces.
- Clan boosts and uploaded clan or player artwork are available to the plugin's side panel.
- Feed achievements use shorter, clearer labels, and ordinary item thumbnails no longer fill Discord's entire thumbnail box.

## Fixed
- Plugin clients can detect and repair an incomplete cached Supporter-name list even when its version number appears current.
