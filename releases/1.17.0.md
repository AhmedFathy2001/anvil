## Highlights
- Supporters can host private bingos and races for friends from anvilosrs.com/host.
- Streamers, and anyone without an in-game clan, can open a creator space and run events for their viewers.
- Every character has one profile page, your settings live in one place, and you can react to drops and deaths on the site.

## New
- Host an event for your friends (Supporter perk): a bingo, solo bingo or race, tracked by the plugin like a clan event. Friends join with your private invite link. Supporters hold up to 10 players at once, Champions 25, and Patrons 50 across 2 live events.
- Creator spaces: host events from anvilosrs.com/host with no in-game clan. Pick a format, start with ready-made tiles or a blank board, and share one link. Viewers join with one button and teams balance themselves.
- Choose who can join a creator event: anyone, your Twitch, Kick or YouTube followers, subscribers or members, or people with a role in your Discord or boosting it. Viewers can also sign up by typing !join in your Twitch or Kick chat.
- A stream overlay for OBS with standings, the feed or the board. It updates live, can celebrate finished tiles, and comes with Join, Board and Standings panels for your Twitch or Kick About section.
- Four overlay looks to choose from: Classic, Glass, Lower third and Pixel.
- Stream highlights: big moments in a streamed event are clipped on Twitch, or saved as a timestamp on YouTube.
- Creator pages: add a logo, banner, bio, info panels, a weekly schedule shown in each visitor's own time, and a featured clip. Page colours can be picked from your logo and banner, for clans too.
- A clan's page shows a Moments wall of its members' best drops, pets and achievements.
- Find events to join at anvilosrs.com/events. Clans and creators can list an event there with "Advertise on Anvil".
- Head-to-head challenges (Supporter perk): challenge a friend from their profile to be first to a drop, or get the most kills or XP in 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days. Anvil tracks you both and calls the winner. Bragging rights only.
- Show off on your profile: Supporters pin their best drops, kill counts and personal bests to a display case (4, 8 or 12 slots by tier), and Champions and Patrons can upload their own avatar and banner, animated if they like.
- A Settings page with every personal setting in one place, and a page for each of your characters.
- Link your Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Medal accounts in Settings and show them on your profile.
- React on the site: GZ on drops, pets and achievements, Sad or Sit on deaths, on a member's Lately list and an event's Moments. Hover a reaction to see who gave it.
- Pick how players get onto teams: auto-balance, players choose their team, or the host drafts as before. A new Solo format gives everyone their own team.
- Clans that stream can turn on Streaming and let viewers into one event without joining the clan.
- Joining a streamed event works on a phone: the link opens straight to one Join button.
- Game modes: clans can give each game mode its own drop and level-up filters, and boards set to count a mode credit its kill counts, XP and log slots, checked against that mode's own hiscores.
- Hardcore Worlds: when a character falls, Anvil posts it as "Fallen" in the mode's channel and the clan's deaths channel, and it shows in the clan feed, where you can react with Sad or Sit.
- A Report button on clan pages, events, profiles, creator pages and highlights sends a report to Anvil staff. The person reported never sees who sent it.
- New guides for streamers: hosting a creator space, joining a streamer's event, and streaming for your clan.

## Improved
- A tidier top bar: your clan's pages on the left, and on the right your teams, one Manage menu for staff, the clan's Discord, and an avatar menu with your profile, settings and sign out. It no longer wraps on smaller screens.
- Event settings are split into tabs with a search box, each tab with its own Save bar, and creator spaces only see the settings that apply to them.
- Settings pages share one cleaner layout: clear section titles, switches and pick-one buttons instead of long dropdowns.
- One character profile everywhere: anvilosrs.com/p, a clan's member page and your own profile now show the same page with all its records. Your profile is a single page, several characters fold into one list, and links and Back keep the tab you were on.
- Your characters show as chips at the top of your profile, one click to switch, and the list of your accounts isn't repeated below.
- Creator plans count how many players are in your events at the same time, so events that overlap share one limit.
- The billing portal shows everything you pay for in one place.
- Boss icons match the ones in RuneLite's hiscores, and item pictures in Discord posts are sharp instead of blurry.
- Your Supporter colours show on your own name in the top bar and on your character names across the site.
- Supporter colours only show on characters proven to be yours by the plugin, everywhere on the site and in game.
- "This is my character" on the site starts the same quick XP check as "This is my account" in the plugin, and you can follow the claim in Settings.
- Progress can only be sent for characters that are yours, and event staff are told when one login plays another login's character.
- Supporter page lists exactly what each tier gets, with a short explanation of friend events and head-to-head.
- Plugin sign-ins show when each was last used, and Link device explains where the code comes from.
- [Plugin] A tile's details list its latest proofs, and you can delete a proof you're allowed to remove.
- [Plugin] A clan board you're on shows once on the Clan tab, with your team.

## Fixed
- On a phone, the top menu opens as a list instead of a sideways strip, and your name no longer wraps under the character count.
- Tab strips and sideways lists no longer show stray scrollbars.
- "Upgrade plan" on the member limit notice opens your clan's Plan and billing page instead of the home page.
- Gold buttons such as Connect your Discord server and Save are readable again.
- Times show the fastest team-size time as the headline, and Tombs of Amascut Expert, Chambers of Xeric CM and Theatre of Blood HM use their own icons.
- Spreadsheets from event archives open as plain text in Excel and Google Sheets.
- The site icon lost a stray white sparkle, and link previews no longer cut off the board picture.
