## Highlights
- Supporters can boost the clans they're in: boosted clans get a badge, a place higher in the Clan Hall and, at higher levels, a moving name, an animated banner and a monthly recruitment post in Anvil's Discord.
- A new Feed tab shows your clan's and boards' big moments live, with chat for each event.
- Supporters' names show in their colours across the game: chat, private messages, clan broadcasts, login notices, friends and clan lists, and the right-click menu.
- One drop now counts on every board you're on.

## New
- Clan boosts: Supporters get 1 boost, Champions 2 and Patrons 3, to place on any clan where one of their characters has been a member for 14 days. Boosts can move once a week. Place them in Settings → Clan boosts.
- Boost levels at 3, 8 and 15 boosts. Level 1: a Boosted badge, a higher place in the Clan Hall and search, and a gradient clan name. Level 2: a featured spot, an animated banner, a recruiting line and a moving name. Level 3: the Clan Hall spotlight and one recruitment post a month in Anvil's Discord, written by the clan. A clan that drops a level keeps its perks for a week.
- Boosters get a mark next to their name in that clan's roster, feed and Discord posts, a place on the clan's boosters wall, and badges for being the first booster, for boosting 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and for the boost that levelled the clan up.
- [Plugin] A Feed tab: live drops, pets, achievements, quests, deaths and finished tiles for your clan and every board you're on. Pick which from a dropdown, filter by kind or your team, and open it straight from a board's page.
- [Plugin] Event chat: talk to everyone on a board, or only your team, from the side panel. How often you can send follows your Supporter tier: every 5 seconds, 3 for Supporters, 1 for Champions, and no wait for Patrons.
- [Plugin] React from the Feed: GZ on drops, pets and achievements, and Sad or Sit on deaths, on clan and board feeds. Hover a reaction to see who gave it.
- [Plugin] "Show feed in game chat": big drops, finished tiles and milestones in your chatbox as they happen, your teammates' progress on item tiles, and a line on where you stand when you log in.
- [Plugin] Supporters' names wear their colours, gradient and Patron star in public, clan, group and private chat (both ways, split chat included), in clan broadcasts and "has logged in" notices, on your friends list and clan member lists, and on your own name in the chat input. Hover a name for their tier. Turn it off with "Supporter names in chat"; turning it off clears the colours at once.
- [Plugin] Supporter colours on player names in the right-click menu, never in PvP areas or on Deadman worlds. They paint over other plugins' name colours such as Player Indicators, or turn "Over other plugins' colours" off to keep theirs. Turn it off with "Supporter names in menus".
- [Plugin] One drop counts on every board you're on, such as your clan's bingo and a clan-vs-clan at the same time.
- [Plugin] Game-mode worlds, Hardcore Worlds included, are recognised: their posts go to that mode's own channels and only count on boards set up for that mode.
- [Plugin] Proof thumbnails under a tile; click one to open the full screenshot.
- [Plugin] New tile kinds: collection log tiles credited the moment the slot unlocks, combo tiles with their parts folded underneath, multi-run timed tiles ("3/5 runs"), and counters for slayer tasks per master, Tithe Farm, Wintertodt, Guardians of the Rift and Doom of Mokhaiotl delves.

## Improved
- Every dropdown, date, checkbox and slider on the site now uses Anvil's own look instead of your browser's.
- In Discord setup, a webhook that isn't Anvil's but that you want to keep can be kept, so it leaves the "remove old webhooks" list.
- [Plugin] Deaths reach the feed in about a second and name what killed you, including poison and venom, and bosses like Alchemical Hydra that never target you.
- [Plugin] Names in the Feed and chat show a Supporter's colours, or else their team's colour, with the team written next to it.
- [Plugin] New Supporter colours reach the game within seconds instead of on the next refresh, and only the changes are sent.
- [Plugin] In event chat, the "your line" highlight marks only your own lines, on every character you play.
- [Plugin] Chat lines stay readable on both the solid and the transparent chatbox, and chat colours you change on the site apply straight away.
- [Plugin] A run of chat lines from one person starts again with their name and time after a 5-minute gap.
- [Plugin] Patron names slide their gradient in the panel, the Feed and event chat. Turn "Animated names" off to keep them still.
- [Plugin] The Feed and chat stay smooth on slower PCs, with a few other speed-ups.
- [Plugin] Refresh shows it's working and reloads the Feed tab too.
- [Plugin] Text that's cut short shows in full when you hover it.
- [Plugin] The Clan tab says Space when you're playing in a creator space, and spaces are marked in the clan dropdown.

## Fixed

- A clan you left no longer shows you its member view.
- Settings pages send you to sign in instead of showing an error when you're signed out.
- In the admin sidebar, Clan settings no longer lights up while you're on Clan events.
- [Plugin] A deleted submission comes off the panel at once instead of coming back.
- [Plugin] Sending a chat line hands the keyboard back to the game.
- [Plugin] Awakened kill tiles no longer count normal kills.
- [Plugin] A failed starting shot or screenshot upload says why in chat.
- [Plugin] Countdowns over a day read "4d 4h", and quotes and dashes no longer show as "?".
- [Plugin] Some board pages were pushed to the right, a live tile count could go past its goal, a tile's proofs could stay on "Loading…", and the Patron star could be missing from the first line after login.
