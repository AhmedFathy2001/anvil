## New
- Supporters and boosted clans can set a separate name look for dark backgrounds and for light ones. When set, it is shown exactly as chosen; otherwise the main colours are adjusted to stay readable.
- Boosted clans can choose their own clan-name colour or gradient.
- Staff can see a supporter's paid tier, any temporary upgrade, who gave it and when it ends.

## Improved
- Gifted clan boosts now top up only what a clan is missing, and step back as real boosts arrive.
- Animated clan and profile artwork loops continuously in the plugin.
- Clan members who are in your clan in game but not yet on the roster are the only plugin players that ask to join.

## Fixed
- Clan icons and player details in the plugin's side panel are no longer cropped.
- Supporter names in the side panel show their full colours instead of white or a single colour.
- "Verified in log" links open the right collection-log page.
- Players on a weekly board with zero kills are no longer told they are not on it.
- Brief placeholder names from the login screen no longer create join requests.
