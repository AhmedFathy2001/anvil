## Efficiency (EHP / EHB) algorithm and rate tables

`src/lib/efficiency.ts` is a faithful port of the EHP/EHB efficiency algorithm, and the rate tables
in `src/data/efficiencyRates.json` and the fast band of `src/data/activityRates.json` are derived
from the same project's published rate configs.

## Game data (drop tables, money-making guides, kill rates — slow band)

Drop-rate tables (`src/data/npcDrops.json`), the slow band of `src/data/activityRates.json`, and
related game datasets are derived from the Old School RuneScape Wiki (via its Bucket API and
money-making guides).
