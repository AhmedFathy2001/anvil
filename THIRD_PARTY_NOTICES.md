in `src/data/efficiencyRates.json` and the fast band of `src/data/activityRates.json` are derived
from the same project's published rate configs.

## Game data (drop tables, money-making guides, kill rates — slow band)

Drop-rate tables (`src/data/npcDrops.json`), the slow band of `src/data/activityRates.json`, and
related game datasets are derived from the Old School RuneScape Wiki (via its Bucket API and
money-making guides).

- **Source:** Old School RuneScape Wiki — <https://oldschool.runescape.wiki>
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 3.0

The item sprites in `public/og/sprites/` (the art on the platform's link card) come from the same
wiki, enlarged without smoothing.

## Fonts on the link cards

`public/og/fonts/` holds the Latin subsets of Fraunces and Geist / Geist Mono, the faces the site
already loads from Google Fonts, bundled so the server can draw them onto link cards.
